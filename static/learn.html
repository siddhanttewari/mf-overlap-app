Questions

Is OverlapIQ Learn free?

Yes. There is no signup, no payment and no PAN. Your progress and journal stay in your own browser.

Does this tell me what to buy or sell?

No. It teaches how markets, risk, charts and trading work, using real Indian market history. It never recommends a stock, fund or trade, and the replay lab uses virtual money only.

What is the replay lab?

A time machine. You get ₹10 lakh of virtual money and replay a real stretch of Nifty 50 history, day by day, without knowing the future. At the end you see how you did against buy-and-hold and an auto-invest plan, and how your own behaviour affected the result.

Where does the data come from?

Daily closing levels of the Nifty 50 price index from NSE Indices, as published by Yahoo Finance, from 17 Sep 2007 to 13 Apr 2026. The price index excludes dividends. Index weights come from the NSE Indices factsheet of 30 Sep 2026.

Where is my progress stored?

Only in your browser on this device. Clearing site data erases it. Nothing about your quiz answers, trades or journal is sent to us.

🔒 Private by design: no signup, no PAN, nothing uploaded. Quiz scores, replay trades and journal entries live in your browser only. How privacy works here

OverlapIQ Learn is for education only. It does not recommend buying, selling or holding any security, and nothing here is investment, tax or legal advice. The replay lab uses virtual money. Charts and figures use daily closing levels of the Nifty 50 price index (NSE Indices) as published by Yahoo Finance; the price index excludes dividends, and past performance does not predict the future.
