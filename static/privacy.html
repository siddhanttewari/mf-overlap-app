Private by design: how OverlapIQ handles your data

No signup, no PAN, no uploads. The amounts you type stay in your browser. Here is exactly what is saved, what is sent, and who else is involved.

On this page:

  • The short version
  • What happens to what you do
  • Who else is involved
  • What we do not do
  • Staying extra private
  • When this page changes

The short version

OverlapIQ is a free tool. You can use it without signing up, giving a PAN, uploading a statement or sharing an email address.

🚫

Nothing to hand over

No signup, no PAN, no folio number, no statement upload.

💻

Your numbers stay with you

The amounts and weights you type are used in your browser. We never receive them and we do not save your portfolio on our servers.

📊

Simple analytics

We use Google Analytics to see which pages help people. It gets counts and clicks, never amounts.

🧒 Like you are 5

Think of a calculator on a table. You type your numbers into it and it shows the answer on its own screen. We never see your numbers. We only see that someone used the calculator.

About "stays in your browser"

Your direct stocks and watchlist are saved in your browser's own storage on this device. That is why they are still there next time. Only you can see them. If you clear your site data, or use a different browser or phone, they are gone. Fund searches and the fund you pick do go to our server, because that is how it finds the holdings to show you.

About the Portfolio X-Ray

If you choose a CSV file or paste a list, it is read by your browser and never uploaded. Your list is saved in your browser's own storage so it is there next time, and the Clear button removes it. The only thing our server receives is the ID of each fund, so it can send back that fund's public holdings.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your PAN, folio numbers, bank details or login.
  • We do not save your portfolio, amounts or weights on our servers.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not send you emails, because we do not ask for your email address.
  • We do not give investment advice. See the notes on each page.

Staying extra private

  • Delete what is saved on this device: clear this site's data in your browser settings, or use a private window.
  • Use a shared computer? Remove your direct stocks before you leave, or use a private window.
  • Do not want analytics? Use the opt-out add-on above.
