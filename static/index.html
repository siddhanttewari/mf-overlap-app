Important notes:

  • Holdings data sourced from MFData.in, an open-source aggregator of SEBI-mandated AMC monthly disclosures.
  • Search supports all 14,500+ schemes across 50+ AMCs (equity, hybrid, debt, etc.). Holdings shown for funds that publish equity portfolios.
  • Overlap formula: Σ min(weight_A, weight_B) over common stocks, with weights normalized to 100%.
  • Only equity holdings are considered for overlap math. Cash, debt, and derivatives are excluded. Not investment advice.
