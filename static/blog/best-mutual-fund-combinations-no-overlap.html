Best Mutual Fund Combinations for Minimal Overlap (2026)

We tested dozens of fund combinations on OverlapIQ to find the portfolios with the lowest overlap and best diversification. Here are 6 data-backed templates for Indian investors.

💡 The golden rule

Diversify across categories, not across AMCs. Two Large Cap funds from different AMCs overlap 65-75%. A Large Cap + Small Cap from the SAME AMC overlaps just 10-15%.

Portfolio 1: The Minimalist (2 Funds)

~12% overlapBest for: beginners, small SIP amounts
  • Nifty 50 Index Fund (50% allocation) — broad large-cap market exposure at lowest cost
  • Mid Cap Fund (50% allocation) — growth potential from a completely different stock universe

Example: UTI Nifty 50 Index + Kotak Emerging Equity. Only ~12% overlap because mid-cap stocks rarely appear in Nifty 50.

Portfolio 2: The Core-Satellite (3 Funds)

~15% avg overlapBest for: moderate risk, tax-saving need
  • Flexi Cap Fund (40%) — core holding with multi-cap flexibility
  • Small Cap Fund (30%) — high-growth, low overlap with large-cap-heavy core
  • ELSS Fund (30%) — tax savings under Section 80C, partially overlaps with core

Example: Parag Parikh Flexi Cap + SBI Small Cap + Mirae Asset Tax Saver. The Parag Parikh international exposure makes it unique. Small Cap has near-zero overlap with the other two.

Portfolio 3: The All-Weather (4 Funds)

~18% avg overlapBest for: ₹20K+ monthly SIP, long-term wealth
  • Nifty 50 Index Fund (30%) — low-cost large cap beta
  • Mid Cap Fund (25%) — active mid-cap alpha
  • Small Cap Fund (25%) — high growth, high risk
  • International/Flexi Cap with global (20%) — geographic diversification

Example: HDFC Index Nifty 50 + Axis Midcap + Nippon Small Cap + Parag Parikh Flexi Cap. Each fund targets a different market segment. Pairwise overlaps range from 5-25%.

Portfolio 4: The Aggressive Growth (5 Funds)

~20% avg overlapBest for: high risk tolerance, 10+ year horizon
  • Flexi Cap Fund (25%) — core multi-cap allocation
  • Mid Cap Fund (20%) — dedicated mid-cap exposure
  • Small Cap Fund (20%) — high-growth potential
  • Sectoral Fund (IT or Pharma) (15%) — concentrated sector bet
  • ELSS Fund (20%) — tax saving + equity

The sectoral fund is the key differentiator — IT or Pharma stocks have near-zero overlap with financial/industrial-heavy diversified funds.

Portfolio 5: The Value Seeker (3 Funds)

~22% avg overlapBest for: contrarian investors, PSU/dividend focus
  • Value/Contra Fund (40%) — contrarian picks, PSU-heavy
  • Mid Cap Fund (30%) — growth from mid-tier companies
  • Nifty Next 50 Index Fund (30%) — exposure to 51st-100th largest companies

Value funds hold different stocks (NTPC, Coal India, ONGC) than growth-oriented mid caps. Nifty Next 50 bridges the gap with emerging large caps.

Portfolio 6: The Tax-Efficient (3 Funds)

~16% avg overlapBest for: maximising 80C + growing wealth
  • ELSS Fund (40%) — ₹1.5L tax deduction under 80C
  • Small Cap Fund (30%) — growth engine, minimal ELSS overlap
  • Nifty Next 50 Index Fund (30%) — low-cost exposure to emerging large caps

ELSS funds are mostly large-cap, so pairing with Small Cap and Next 50 gives exposure to very different parts of the market.

Combinations to Avoid

  • 🚫 Two Large Cap funds from any AMCs — 60-80% overlap guaranteed
  • 🚫 Large Cap + Flexi Cap from same AMC — 55-70% overlap
  • 🚫 Nifty 50 Index + Active Large Cap — you're paying higher fees for 70% of the same portfolio
  • 🚫 Three Flexi Cap funds — all converge to the same large-cap stocks
  • 🚫 ELSS + Large Cap + Flexi Cap — triple overlap on HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Reliance
