Best Mutual Fund Combinations for Low Overlap in India (2026)

Most investors pick funds by returns alone, ignoring whether they actually diversify each other. We used OverlapIQ's data to build 5 model portfolios where every fund pair has minimal overlap — meaning every rupee you invest goes to genuinely different stocks.

📊 Key Data Point

Our model portfolios demonstrate that cross-category combinations achieve maximum overlap of 20-28% between any fund pair, compared to 60-80% for same-category combinations. The lowest-overlap 3-fund portfolio (Parag Parikh + Mid Cap + Small Cap) averages just 15% pairwise overlap.

The Golden Rule: Cross Categories, Not AMCs

The single most important principle: diversification comes from mixing different fund categories, not different AMCs. Two Large Cap funds from HDFC and ICICI overlap 70%. One Large Cap fund and one Small Cap fund from the same AMC overlap just 12%. Category is what matters.

Combination 1: The Minimalist (2 Funds)

MAX OVERLAP: ~18%
UTI Nifty 50 Index Fund (60%)Large Cap core
Axis Midcap Fund (40%)Mid cap growth

Why it works: Index fund covers the top 50 stocks passively. Mid Cap fund invests in companies ranked 101-250 — almost zero stock overlap. You get broad market exposure at the lowest combined expense ratio.

Combination 2: The Core-Satellite (3 Funds)

MAX OVERLAP: ~22%
Parag Parikh Flexi Cap Fund (40%)Core — India + global
Kotak Emerging Equity Fund (35%)Satellite — mid cap
SBI Small Cap Fund (25%)Satellite — small cap

Why it works: Parag Parikh's US holdings (Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon — roughly 12% of portfolio) have zero overlap with any Indian fund. The mid and small cap satellites invest in entirely different market segments. Three funds, three genuinely different portfolios.

Combination 3: The Tax-Efficient (3 Funds)

MAX OVERLAP: ~25%
Mirae Asset Tax Saver (ELSS) (40%)Tax saving + large cap
HDFC Mid-Cap Opportunities Fund (35%)Mid cap growth

Why it works: ELSS doubles as your large cap allocation while saving tax under Section 80C. The mid and small cap funds target different market segments with minimal stock overlap. Total Section 80C benefit: up to ₹1.5 lakh deduction.

Combination 4: The All-Weather (4 Funds)

Combinations to Avoid

✗Two Large Cap funds from different AMCs — 60-80% overlap guaranteed
✗Large Cap + Flexi Cap from the same AMC — the Flexi Cap is usually 65% large cap stocks
✗ELSS + Large Cap + Flexi Cap together — triple overlap on the same blue chips
✗Multiple Index funds tracking the same index — literally 99% overlap, zero diversification
✗Focused fund + same AMC's Large Cap — focused funds are concentrated versions of the same portfolio

How to Build Your Own Low-Overlap Portfolio

You don't need to follow these exact combinations. The principle is simple: pick funds from different categories, check the overlap, and adjust. Here's the process:

Step 1: Decide your market cap allocation (e.g., 40% large, 30% mid, 30% small).

Step 2: Pick one fund per category — the best in each category by returns + expense ratio.

Step 3: Run them through OverlapIQ to verify the overlap is below 30% between every pair.

Step 4: If any pair exceeds 40%, replace one fund with a different category entirely.
